# RoyaltyNexus Foundation — Website Marketing Strategy

This is the strategy the homepage is built on. Change it here first, then change the site to match.

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** RoyaltyNexus Foundation turns private wealth into permanent, measurable good for the planet.

**What makes us different**
- **Permanence.** Gifts can be endowed, so they keep funding work for generations.
- **Stewardship.** Funds are invested under a written, fossil-free policy, with an independent investment committee.
- **Proof.** Every grant has field targets (hectares, litres, homes, tonnes CO₂e), and we report against them every year.
- **Global reach, local partners.** We fund organisations rooted in the places they restore.

**Tone:** confident, calm and precise, like a private bank, with warmth about the cause. We never use guilt, hype or invented numbers.

## 2. Audiences, in priority order

| Audience | What they want | What we offer them | Main call to action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Families, founders, family offices (high net worth) | A legacy, control, discretion, proof | Named endowment and donor-advised funds, legacy gifts, a private briefing | **Request a private briefing** |
| Individual donors | To know their money works | Donations to a programme or where needed most, clear spending split | **Donate** |
| Companies (CSR / ESG) | Credible climate and community partners, reporting | Co-funded programmes, employee volunteering, impact data | **Partner with us** |
| Volunteers and students | To take part, recognition | Campaigns with verifiable certificates | **Join a campaign** |

## 3. Message map

- **Headline promise:** "Wealth that endures. A planet that thrives."
- **Proof points, repeated across the page:** independent board · annual external audit · fossil-free endowment · donor privacy · targets published openly.
- **Objections answered in the FAQ:** Is it tax-deductible? How is my fund invested? Can I stay anonymous? How much reaches programmes? Can I give from another country? What happens if returns fall?

## 4. Page journey

1. **Hero:** the promise, two calls to action (private briefing, donate) and trust chips.
2. **Choose your path:** one card per audience, so each visitor self-selects within seconds.
3. **Mission:** three pillars (restore the Earth · steward wealth · grow communities).
4. **How stewardship works:** four steps from gift to grants.
5. **Transparency:** where each 100 goes (spending policy) and 2030 targets.
6. **Programmes:** six global programmes, each with its unit of measurement.
7. **Governance and documents:** standing rules and the reports we publish.
8. **Founding Circle:** an invitation for the first families who endow named funds.
9. **FAQ:** answers to objections.
10. **Contact:** a short form with consent, and direct email addresses.

Calls to action repeat at the hero, after stewardship, at the Founding Circle and in the menu (Give).

## 5. Rules the website follows

These are the conventions donors, regulators and charity watchdogs expect from a trust or foundation website.

- **No invented facts.** No made-up statistics, testimonials, partner logos or awards. Numbers are labelled as targets or policy until audited results exist.
- **Transparency.** The site shows how funds are spent, who governs, and which reports are published.
- **Investment disclaimers.** Charitable gifts are irrevocable. Endowment values can fall as well as rise. Nothing on the site is investment advice or an offer of securities. Payout rates are policy, not promises.
- **Tax wording by country.** We only say a gift is tax-deductible once registration in that country is confirmed. Until then the site says that relief depends on the donor's country.
- **Registration details.** Charity or trust registration and tax-exemption numbers go in the footer once issued.
- **Privacy and consent.** Forms ask for consent and link to the privacy policy (`privacy.html`). Donor data is never sold or shared. Visitors can ask to see, correct or delete their data. This follows the main points of GDPR, India's DPDP Act and similar laws.
- **Fraud warning.** The site states that we never ask for gifts through personal accounts or messaging apps.
- **Accessibility.** WCAG 2.2 AA: readable contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion respected, text alternatives.
- **Search and sharing.** Title, description, Open Graph tags and schema.org `NGO` data are on the homepage.

## 6. Measuring success

| Goal | Metric |
|---|---|
| Wealth conversations | Private briefing requests per month |
| Giving | Donations and average gift |
| Partnerships | Company enquiries |
| Participation | Campaign registrations and certificates issued |
| Trust | Downloads of the annual report and audited accounts |
