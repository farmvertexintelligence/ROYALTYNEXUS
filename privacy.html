Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy is a starting draft; have it reviewed by a lawyer in each country where the foundation is registered before relying on it.

In short: we collect only what we need to reply to you, process your gift and keep you informed if you ask us to. We never sell or rent your data. You can ask us to show, correct or delete it at any time.

Who we are

RoyaltyNexus Foundation ("we") is a non-profit charitable organisation. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: hello@royaltynexus.org.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: name, email, phone, country, your area of interest and your message.
  • Gifts: details needed to process and receipt a donation, and any preferences such as anonymity.
  • Campaigns: registration details and attendance, to issue verifiable service certificates.
  • News: your email address, if you subscribe.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you and arrange briefings, gifts or partnerships.
  • To issue receipts and meet legal, tax and audit duties.
  • To send reports and news, only if you asked for them.

We rely on your consent, on steps you ask us to take, and on our legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Sharing

We never sell, rent or trade donor or visitor data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the foundation (for example hosting, payments or email), under contracts that protect it, or where the law requires it.

Storage and international transfers

As a global foundation we may store data in more than one country. Where we do, we use safeguards required by applicable law. We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above and for legal record-keeping.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Forms may use your browser's storage to work. If we add analytics, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or similar laws), you can ask to:

  • see the data we hold about you,
  • correct or update it,
  • delete it,
  • stop receiving news, or withdraw consent,
  • complain to your data protection authority.

Email hello@royaltynexus.org and we will respond within 30 days.

Donor rights

  • To know how your gift will be used, and to receive a receipt for it.
  • To see our annual report, audited accounts and governance rules.
  • To give anonymously, and to have your details kept private.
  • To ask questions and receive prompt, honest answers.
  • Never to be pressured to give.
