Private wealth stewardship

From a single gift to grants for generations

We are a non-profit and charge donors no fees. Every return the endowment earns, after audited operating costs, goes to our charitable mission.

  1. You commit capital

    Give outright, establish a named fund, or leave a legacy gift. We agree the fund's purpose with you in writing.

  2. We invest responsibly

    Your fund joins our endowment pool, invested for long-term growth with no fossil-fuel, tobacco or weapons exposure.

  3. Returns fund the mission

    Each year a prudent share, around 4–5% of the fund's average value, is granted out, so the principal can keep its real value.

  4. You see the impact

    Every grant carries field targets. You receive an annual statement for your fund and a report on what it achieved.

Questions

What donors ask us

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Tax relief depends on the country you pay tax in and where the foundation is registered. We confirm eligibility in writing before you give, and issue an official receipt for every gift. Our registration and tax-exemption details are listed in the footer as each one is issued.

How is my named fund invested?

Named funds are pooled in our endowment under a written investment policy: diversified, long-term and fossil-free, overseen by an independent investment committee. Values can fall as well as rise, so payouts are set as a share of the three-year average value to keep grants steady.

How much of my gift reaches programmes?

Our spending policy targets 85 of every 100 for programmes. Audited figures are published each year so you can check us against it.

Can I stay anonymous?

Yes. You can give anonymously or name a fund after a cause instead of your family. We never sell or share donor details.

Can I give from another country?

Yes, we welcome gifts from around the world. Contact us first so we can confirm the right route for your country and the paperwork you will need.

Is this investment advice?

No. RoyaltyNexus Foundation is a charity. Gifts to it are charitable and irrevocable, not investments, and you receive no financial return. Please take independent advice on your own finances and taxes.
