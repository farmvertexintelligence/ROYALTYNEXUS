Private wealth, held in trust for a living planet.

RoyaltyNexus Foundation helps families and individuals turn their wealth into permanent public good. We steward endowed capital responsibly and use every rupee and dollar of its returns to restore forests, rivers and coasts, and to help communities grow without costing the Earth.

What we do

Three commitments, one purpose

We exist to keep the Earth habitable and prosperous for the generations after ours. Our work rests on three connected pillars.

Pillar I

Protect and restore the Earth

We fund science-led restoration of the ecosystems that hold our climate steady and our water clean.

  • Native forest and mangrove restoration
  • Watershed and wetland revival
  • Biodiversity corridors and seed banks
Pillar II

Steward private wealth for public good

Donors place capital in permanent or time-bound endowment funds that we invest under a strict responsible-investment policy.

  • Endowment and donor-advised funds
  • Legacy and family philanthropy planning
  • Fossil-free, impact-screened portfolios
Pillar III

Help the world grow, sustainably

We back people and enterprises that create livelihoods while lowering pressure on land, water and air.

  • Clean energy for rural homes and schools
  • Green livelihoods and women-led enterprise
  • Climate education for young people
How wealth stewardship works

From a gift today to grants for generations

We are a non-profit. We do not earn fees from donors' wealth. Every return the endowment earns, after audited operating costs, goes to our charitable mission.

  1. You commit capital

    Give outright, set up a named fund for your family, or leave a legacy gift in your will. We agree the fund's purpose with you in writing.

  2. We invest it responsibly

    The fund joins our endowment pool, invested for long-term growth with no fossil-fuel, tobacco or weapons exposure, overseen by an independent investment committee.

  3. Returns fund the mission

    Each year a prudent share of the fund's value, around 4–5%, is paid out as grants, so the principal keeps its real value over time.

  4. Impact is measured and reported

    Every grant has field targets in hectares restored, litres recharged, homes powered and tonnes of CO₂e avoided. You receive an annual report for your fund.

Programmes

Where the money goes to work

We fund local partners who know their land best, and we stay for the years it takes a forest or a river to recover.

Coasts · Mangroves

Blue Carbon Coasts

Replanting mangroves and seagrass that shelter fishing villages from storms and lock away carbon several times faster than land forests.

Measured in hectares replanted and seedling survival rate
Rivers · Watersheds

Living Rivers

Reviving tanks, check dams and wetlands with farming communities so rain stays in the ground and wells refill.

Measured in million litres of groundwater recharged
Land · Native forests

Native Forest Corridors

Growing native species, never monoculture plantations, to reconnect fragmented habitats for wildlife and pollinators.

Measured in native trees surviving after three years
Energy · Rural access

Bright Villages

Solar power for homes, schools and clinics, with local technicians trained and paid to maintain the systems.

Measured in households and schools powered
People · Livelihoods

Green Livelihoods

Seed grants and mentoring for women-led and youth-led enterprises in regenerative farming, recycling and eco-tourism.

Measured in jobs created and income earned
Youth · Education

Earth Classrooms

Hands-on climate and nature education, school nurseries and field projects that turn students into local stewards.

Measured in students and schools reached

Governance & transparency

Trust is the asset we protect most

A foundation that manages other people's wealth must be easy to check. These are our standing rules.

Board of trustees
A majority of independent trustees. No trustee may receive grants or contracts from the foundation.
Investment committee
Independent finance professionals set and review the investment policy every year.
Audit
Annual statutory audit by an external firm, published in full on this site.
Cost discipline
Administrative costs are capped and reported as a share of annual spending.
Donor privacy
Donor details are never sold or shared. Gifts can be anonymous on request.
Conflict of interest
Every trustee and staff member declares interests annually and steps aside where one exists.
