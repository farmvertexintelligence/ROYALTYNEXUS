Our foundation

A public trust for the living world.

The Royalty Nexus Foundation Trust is a public charitable trust. Every campaign is open to all — students, families, professionals and companies — and free to join. We work on the ground in forests, rivers and coasts, and online through live classes and citizen-science programmes that anyone can join from home.

What we restore

Four pillars of environmental action

Reforestation

Native-species planting, seed banks and multi-year survival monitoring of every sapling.

Clean Water

River clean-ups, wetland revival and citizen water-quality testing along urban rivers.

Wildlife & Coasts

Mangrove belts, habitat corridors and nesting-site protection with local communities.

Climate Education

Free online masterclasses, school programmes and practical toolkits for climate action.
